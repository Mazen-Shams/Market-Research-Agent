## 📊 Key Analytical Findings

1. **€1.99M Operational Loss Exposure**:
   * Analysis of 1,500 monitored cycles across 87 presses identified **52 cycle aborts** (3.47% failure rate), resulting in **85 scrapped heavy commercial TBR tyres** and **5,222 minutes (87.0 hours) of unplanned downtime**.
   * Downtime represents **98.4% of total financial losses (€1,958,250)** at standard €22,500/hour line capacity valuation.
2. **Line Concentration (80/20 Rule)**:
   * **Lines E and F account for 41.5% of total plant losses (€825,745)** due to aged first-generation hydraulic power pack motors. Adding Line C accounts for **54.4% of all plant losses across just 3 of 10 lines**.
3. **Primary Mechanical Bottleneck**:
   * **Hydraulic Power Pack Motors** cause **55.3% of all scrapped tyres (47 tyres)**, followed by **Main Power Supplies (24.7%)**.
4. **Zero False-Negative Thermal Diagnostic Window**:
   * **100% of cycle aborts occurred at $\Delta T \ge 26.0^\circ\text{C}$** above ambient (mean failure $\Delta T = 38.6^\circ\text{C}$ vs $11.3^\circ\text{C}$ normal). **Zero aborts occurred when $\Delta T < 26.0^\circ\text{C}$**, providing an actionable **30 to 90-minute predictive early-warning window** before hydraulic pressure collapses (52.6 Bar drop).
