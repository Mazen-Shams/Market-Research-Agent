## 📊 Key Analytical Findings

1. **€1.99M Documented 4-Month Loss Exposure (~€6.1M Annualized)**:
   * Analysis of 1,500 inspections across 87 presses (Jan–Apr 2026) identified **52 cycle interruptions** (3.47% failure rate), resulting in **85 scrapped heavy commercial TBR tyres** (€32,725) and **5,222 minutes (87.0 hours)** of unplanned downtime (€1,958,250, or **98.4% of total losses**).
2. **Line Concentration (Lines E & F Epicentre)**:
   * **Lines E and F account for 42.3% of failures (22/52)** and **41.5% of total plant losses (€825,745)**. Hydraulic Power Pack Motors drive **51.9% of failures (27/52)** and **55.3% of scrapped tyres (47/85)**.
3. **Sharp Predictive Boundary at $\Delta T \ge 26^\circ\text{C}$**:
   * **0.0% failures** occurred below $\Delta T = 26^\circ\text{C}$ (1,436 inspections below 25°C had zero failures), whereas failures spiked to **78.6%–100%** above threshold (mean failure $\Delta T = 38.6^\circ\text{C}$ vs. $11.3^\circ\text{C}$ normal), paired with a **52.6 Bar** hydraulic pressure drop and **3× vibration spike**.
4. **Verified External Signals (Amazon Quick Research — 27 Sources)**:
   * Natural rubber prices rose **~20% YoY above USD 2.41/kg** in Sept 2026 (approaching a 13-year high, *World Bank / Trading Economics*) amid a 5th straight annual deficit (15.6M MT demand vs. 15.2M MT supply, *ANRPC / Rubber World*).
   * Predictive maintenance delivers **45% less unplanned downtime** (400 → 220 hrs/yr), **66% fewer failures**, **OEE improvement from 65.5% to 75.1%**, **12.4%–41.2% scrap reduction**, and **95% positive ROI** (*DergiPark 2025, IJERD, NIST, McKinsey*).
