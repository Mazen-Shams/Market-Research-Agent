## Executive Summary

Commercial truck tire manufacturers face a convergence of rising natural rubber costs and curing press reliability challenges that makes predictive maintenance a strategically essential investment. Natural rubber prices approached a 13-year high by September 2026, exceeding USD 2.41/kg — roughly 20% above year-earlier levels — with global demand projected to outstrip production for the fifth consecutive year at approximately 15.6 million metric tons versus 15.2 million metric tons of output. Because raw materials constitute 60–65% of a tire company's cost base and natural rubber alone accounts for nearly half of that, these supply-driven price pressures are structurally compressing margins with limited near-term relief.

Predictive maintenance for vulcanization curing presses offers the most accessible lever to offset this cost pressure. IoT-enabled sensor networks and machine learning models — particularly XGBoost, which achieves 96% failure prediction accuracy — have demonstrated a 45% reduction in unplanned downtime, a 67% improvement in mean time between failures, and equipment lifespan extension from 8 to 12 years compared to reactive approaches. At the plant level, Overall Equipment Effectiveness improved from 65.5% to 75.1%, accompanied by a 12.4% scrap rate reduction — directly curtailing waste of increasingly expensive rubber compounds.

The financial case is compelling: a typical predictive maintenance deployment for a mid-sized facility costs $80,000–$180,000 in the first year, with 95% of organizations reporting positive ROI and 27% achieving full payback within 12 months. In an environment where proactive repairs cost four to five times less than emergency interventions, and where every defective tire magnifies the impact of elevated raw material costs, predictive maintenance transforms a volatile cost environment into a manageable operational challenge.

   * Equipment reference: HF Curemaster (Harburg-Freudenberger hydraulic presses).

   * Sensor hierarchy: Vibration (0.35 importance), Temperature (0.30), Pressure (0.20).
